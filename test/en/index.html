L1

Data Cleanup

Laying the foundation — the precondition for everything after it
Your problemWhat we do
The same customer, the same part number, written several ways across files
Master data standardisation.Customers, suppliers, products and part numbers are de-duplicated and named consistently, with a unique identifier so each record points to exactly one thing.
Every department keeps its own Excel and the numbers never reconcile
Inventory the data, then name a single source of truth.We establish where data lives, who maintains it and how often it is updated, then designate one authoritative source and assign responsibility for it.
You want analysis, but eighty percent of the time goes into preparing the data
Quality rules and automated cleansing.We define what counts as valid data and how gaps are filled, then turn the repetitive tidying into a process that can be re-run.
A system is being replaced or upgraded and the old data will not migrate cleanly
Historical data conversion and migration.Field mapping, format conversion, and before-and-after reconciliation to confirm the migrated figures match the originals.
You cleaned it up once, and three months later it was a mess again
Master data maintenance SOP.Rules for additions and changes, who is allowed to edit, and how often it is reviewed, so the clean state holds.
Deliverables
Data inventory, standardised master files, gap list, maintenance rules
Duration
1–4 weeks
L2

API Integration

Connecting the pipes — no need to replace your systems, only to get them talking
Your problemWhat we do
Three systems that do not talk to each other, with manual copy-paste in between
System-to-system integration.We start with an inventory and a feasibility assessment, then build the data paths between ERP, POS, e-commerce platforms, accounting software and CRM.
Marketplace orders are downloaded by hand and imported into ERP
Automated order retrieval and format conversion.Orders are fetched on a schedule, mapped and converted, then written straight into ERP with no one touching them.
You want to use a cloud service but do not know whether it will connect
Integration feasibility assessment.We confirm whether the other side has an API and whether the data can actually come out, assessing risk and timeline before you commit.
The other system is old and has no API at all
Alternative channels.Automated file exchange, direct database reads or web automation, so you do not replace an entire system just to connect it.
A customer or supplier wants data exchange and you do not know where to begin
Define the specification and build the channel.Format, frequency and transport are agreed, with error handling, automatic retries and exception alerts.
Deliverables
Integration architecture diagram, working connections, exception handling, technical documentation
Duration
2–5 weeks
L3

Process Digitalisation & Automation

Fitting the engine — improve first, then automate. We do not move a bad process onto a system untouched
Your problemWhat we do
Approvals, purchase requests and leave are on paper, and no one can tell where a document is stuck
Paper processes taken online.Forms move online and approvals become a system flow, with a record of who handled each step and when, and automatic reminders when something overruns.
The same information is copied into several forms and systems, and transcription errors surface only afterwards
Enter it once, use it throughout.The form itself is the data source and goes straight into the system, with required fields and format checks stopping errors as they are typed.
A document crosses departments and days are lost simply waiting for signatures
Shorten the path.Approval levels and conditional routing are redesigned so small or routine cases clear automatically and only exceptions need a person.
Tracing a document from six months ago means digging through paper files
Searchable, auditable process records.Documents and approval histories are kept together and searchable, so audits, account checks and complaint tracing can be pulled up at once.
The same few days each month, the whole company repeats the same tidying and reconciliation
Automate the highest-frequency work first.Month-end reconciliation, report generation, data consolidation and batch uploads are handed to schedules and scripts that finish on their own.
A process passes five people and seven forms, and nobody can explain why any more
Process mapping and redesign.We map what actually happens, find the rework, waiting and dead steps, remove what is unnecessary, and only then decide which parts are worth automating.
The overtime goes into tidying data rather than doing business
Give the tidying to the machine.RPA, scripts and cloud workflow tools take over the routine, leaving people to judge exceptions and give final confirmation.
A critical task rests on one senior employee, and stops when they are on leave
Turn personal know-how into a written process.The rules become documentation and automation, with a manual and training, so anyone can pick the work up.
Automation was implemented before, broke when the system changed, and no one could fix it
Maintainable build, with ongoing support.Room to adjust is designed in from the start, with post-upgrade tuning and remote technical support.
Deliverables
Current-state process maps and improvement proposals, online forms and approval flows, automations, operating manuals, training
Duration
2–8 weeks
L4

Dashboard Design & Business Insight

Fitting the instruments — ask the right question before drawing the chart
Your problemWhat we do
There are plenty of reports, yet the answer you need still has to be hunted down
Start with a decision interview.We establish the three questions you genuinely need answered daily and monthly, and build only the screens that answer them.
You learn how last month went only after the accounts close, too late to react
Automatic refresh.Data syncs daily and key figures are available at any time, so you are not waiting on the close to find out what happened.
You do not know which customer, product or salesperson actually makes money
Measures derived from the business logic.Customer concentration, product margin, receivables ageing, inventory turnover and capacity-versus-lead-time analyses.
A BI tool is in place but hardly anyone opens the result
Documented definitions, plus regular interpretation.Not just charts, but an account of what happened, the likely cause, and what we suggest doing next.
Everyone arrives with a different number and meetings start by arguing over definitions
One agreed definition per measure.Calculation method, data source and period definitions are written down so the whole company computes the same way.
Deliverables
Measure definitions, dashboards, automatic refresh, regular review sessions
Duration
3–6 weeks
